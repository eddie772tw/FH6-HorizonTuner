## 2024-05-18 - A11y Form Label Alignment in Settings/Params\n**Learning:** Discovered inputs in dynamically generated React forms without proper `htmlFor`/`id` linking or `aria-label`s for min/max ranged inputs, causing silent failures for screen readers navigating tuning sliders and parameter editors.\n**Action:** Always link form labels to their inputs using `htmlFor` matching the input `id`. For compound components (e.g., dual min/max inputs under one visual label), assign discrete `aria-label`s directly to the inputs.
